# Pipeline 2: News Feed & Guardian Magazine Pipeline

**Owner:** Managing Editor + Campus Correspondents + CTO (Web Staging)  
**Output:** Canonical 800–1,200 word web article + Pinterest discovery card + 3–5 min / 13-min audio bulletin.

---

## 1. Story Production Gate Checklist
- [ ] **Two-Source Rule:** ≥2 independent primary sources or certified public records attached.
- [ ] **Right of Reply:** 24–48 hour response window logged for named subjects.
- [ ] **70/30 AI Check:** Human-reported, human-verified.
- [ ] **Category Tag:** Assigned to one of the 9 beats (matches Onboarding Category Picker).

---

## 2. News Feed Article Template

