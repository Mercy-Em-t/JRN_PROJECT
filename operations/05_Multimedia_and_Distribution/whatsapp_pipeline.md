# Pipeline 3: WhatsApp Channel Posting Pipeline

**Owner:** Managing Editor + CTO (OpenGraph & Link Tracking)  
**Output:** High-click OpenGraph preview cards pushed at **07:30 EAT** (Morning Brief) and **13:00 EAT** (Midday Alert).

---

## 1. WhatsApp Channel Drop Rules
1. **Zero Wall-of-Text:** Max 2–3 short lines above the OpenGraph preview card.
2. **Always Include Emoji Reaction Prompt:** Drives algorithmic visibility inside WhatsApp Channels without requiring replies.
3. **Always Link to Web / Audio Dock:** Keeps WhatsApp lightweight while converting readers to the Magazine & M-Pesa patronage flow.

---

## 2. WhatsApp Channel Post Template

```markdown
### [WA-ID] Drop Slot: [07:30 EAT Morning | 13:00 EAT Midday]
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Linked Story ID:** (e.g., `story-1`)
- **Card Headline (≤65 chars):**
- **Card Description (≤110 chars):**
- **2-Line Message Copy:**
  > 🔥 *[Punchy 1-line hook]*
  > 📖 Read the 4-min breakdown or 🎧 listen on your commute: [Link]
- **Reaction Prompt:** *(e.g., "React ❤️ if you use M-Pesa Till or 💡 if you learned something new")*
```
