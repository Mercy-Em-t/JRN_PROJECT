## 1. COPE 3-Asset Packaging Standards
Every approved investigation is packaged into:
1. **Web Longform:** 800–1,200 word article + pull-quotes + data infographic.
2. **Short-Form Video:** 60-second vertical (9:16) cut with burned-in captions, 3-second hook, and Kikao360 watermark (using royalty-free/licensed audio under Kenya Copyright Act Cap 130).
3. **Audio Bulletin:** 3–5 minute daily voice dispatch (or 13-minute flagship weekly deep-dive) formatted for the persistent NPR-style web dock.
