# Department 04: Commercial, Partnerships & Reader Revenue

**Owner:** Commercial & Partnerships Director (Core Founder #4 — KES 20,000/mo stipend)  
**Mandate:** 4-pillar revenue execution, sponsor relationships, Lipa na M-Pesa patronage, and the editorial-commercial firewall.

---

## 1. Four-Pillar Revenue Model (Task 4 Alignment)
1. **Native Brand Sponsorships (45%):** Fintech & enterprise explainers (Safaricom, KCB, Equity).
2. **M-Pesa Reader Patronage (25%):** Voluntary KES 100–1,000 micro-donations via STK Push.
3. **Content Licensing & Syndication (15%):** Licensing investigative video/audio briefs to local radio & digital outlets.
4. **Campus Brand Activations & Job Board (15%):** Sponsored campus hackathons and career listings.

## 2. Editorial–Commercial Firewall Rules
- All sponsored articles, cards, and videos **must** carry a visible **"Sponsored / Partner Content"** badge.
- Sponsors **never** receive pre-publication review or veto rights over independent investigative journalism.
- Reader phone numbers collected via M-Pesa are **never** sold or shared with sponsors.
