**Owner:** Managing Editor & Multimedia Lead (Core Founder #2 — KES 20,000/mo stipend)

**Mandate:** Story commissioning, Two-Source verification, libel defense, and editorial quality assurance.

---

## 1. Pre-Publication 3-Gate Checklist
Every story must pass these gates before going to `03_Product_and_Engineering` or `05_Multimedia_and_Distribution`:
- [ ] **Gate 1 — Two-Source Rule:** At least 2 independent primary sources or certified public records attached in story folder.
- [ ] **Gate 2 — Right of Reply:** Any named individual/institution given 24–48 hours to respond; response (or refusal) documented.
- [ ] **Gate 3 — Legal & Libel Review:** Checked against Kenya *Defamation Act (Cap 36)* and *Computer Misuse & Cybercrimes Act (2018)*.
- [ ] **Gate 4 — 70/30 AI Check:** AI used only for transcription/grammar/summarization; 100% of reporting and conclusions human-verified.

## 1B. Community Tipline (User-Submitted News) Verification Gate
User-submitted tips from the prototype's **📢 Submit Tip** modal or WhatsApp tipline never publish raw:
1. **EXIF & Reverse-Image/Video Check:** Verify media wasn't recycled from old events.
2. **Two-Source Corroboration:** Fact-Check Desk confirms with at least one independent source/official memo.
3. **Public Labeling:** Published as **"Community Dispatch — Verified by Kikao360"**.

## 3. Corrections Protocol
- Never silently edit factual errors.
- Add a timestamped **Editor's Correction Note** at the top of the web article and push a correction card to the WhatsApp Channel within **12 hours**.
