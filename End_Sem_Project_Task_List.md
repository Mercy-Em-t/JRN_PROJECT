* What is their purpose?

### 5. Artificial Intelligence
* What role does it play?
* What is its impact?
* Why is it here?
* How does it contribute to change in the media industry?

### 6. Audience Attention
* What is it?
* Metrics
* Influenced by what?
* How to quickly lose it vs. how to quickly gain it
* Retention?
* Benefits, costs
* Causes of competition
* What does the competition look like?

### 7. Compare & Contrast: Traditional Media Organisations vs. Emerging Digital Media Enterprises
* What are they, who makes them?
* How does each come about?
* Similarities? Differences?
* How do they stay competitive, financially stable, and relevant to the audience?
* How do they operate, when?
* What does the landscape look like for them?

### 8. Innovations
* Innovations to:
  * Stay competitive
  * Stay financially sustainable
  * Stay relevant to audiences
* **Case studies on (7):** How do traditional media achieve this innovation? Compare with that of digital media.

### 9. Role: Media Entrepreneur
* Who are they?
* Characteristics, principles, guardrails
* Job description & qualifications

## Phase IV: Assignment Tasks (Steps 21–32)

> **Gate:** *TASKS — Do not begin unless Steps 1–20 are done satisfactorily.*

### Task 1: Concept (Steps 21–22)

#### 21. Concept Development
* **a) Proposed Venture:** Develop & explain proposed venture using info from Steps 1–20.
* **b) Name & Brand Identity:**
  * What constitutes a brand identity?
  * How did we develop and come up with this?
  * Iterate and return the brand identity backed by info.
* **c) Description of Product or Service (Features):**
  * Brainstorm features using systemic thinking and creative thinking.
  * Categorize: *must-haves, should-haves, could-haves, might-want-to-have*.
  * Narrow down feature list to its essentials; iterate until you have a handful of features.
  * **Return:** (1) All features, (2) Features to include & why.
* **d) Problem, Info Gap, or Market Opportunity:**
  * Audience problem, info gap, or market opportunity it addresses and its justification.
  * **Return:** Justification & response to (d). Iterate if necessary.
* **e) Product's Unique Value Proposition (UVP):**
  * Check 21(c) and apply the same process/context; return UVP.
* **f) Differentiation from Existing Media Offerings (Set-Theory Analysis):**
  * Compare existing media offerings (`A`), our project/product (`B`), within the overall universe (`U`).
  * **Return:**
    * a) $B'$
    * b) $B' \cup A$
    * c) $B \cap A'$
    * d) $A' \cap B'$

#### 22. Output Clear Product Concept
* Name
* Tagline → Demo how it was received
* Basic visual identity & brand mockup

### Task 2: Market Research & Audience Analytics (Steps 23–28)

> **⛔ GATE:** *Do not start if `!(1–22)`.*

#### 23. Preliminary Market Research
* Probe demand & potential audience for the product or service.

#### 24. Identify & Profile Primary & Secondary Target Audience
* **Work backwards:**
  * Think of potential audiences, then narrow down and prepare for these.
  * Why are they the target?
  * What is the correlation or correspondence between who they are and what we are looking for?
  * Why them?
* Run profiling on chosen targets and **return the profile categorised by primary or secondary**.

#### 25. Audience & Competitor Deep Dive (Continued from 24)
* **Audience characteristics:** Interests, media consumption habits, unmet needs.
* **Analysis of existing competitors:**
  * Why are they our primary competitors?
  * How do they beat us?
  * Where do we beat them?
  * Where is a fair playing ground?
  * What can we learn from them?
  * What should we leave behind?

#### 26. Identification of Competitive Advantage
* What is our moat?

#### 27. Evidence of Audience Demand
* Credible secondary research.
* Primary instruments (create them + prepare ways to ingest & process feedback):
  * Interviews
  * Surveys
  * Focus groups

#### 28. Task 2 Gate & Deliverable
* Ensure Steps 1–27 are done.
* **Report on:** Audience persona, Competitor analysis, Evidence supporting market opportunity.
